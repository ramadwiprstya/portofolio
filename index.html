About Me

I am an Information Systems student at Telkom University, Jakarta Campus, with a strong interest in software development, particularly websites and web-based applications.

I have experience developing various information technology projects, including a school attendance system, FitTrack for daily calorie tracking, a commercial property company profile website for Beltway Office Park, a To-Do List application, a financial tracking application, a school complaint management system, and a drought monitoring system in Gunungkidul.

I also have professional experience as a UI/UX Designer at PT Telkom DCS and as a Full-Stack Developer Intern at PT Imeco Inter Saran, within the SBU Property and Infrastructure, focusing on commercial property website development.

I am passionate about building functional, user-friendly digital solutions that address real user needs. I continuously develop my technical skills, explore new technologies, and collaborate with others to deliver effective information systems.
